# Beauty Niche

## Purpose

Define Beauty-specific creative and realism rules for UGC Affiliate content.

## Core Formats

- GRWM
- Tutorial
- Product Application
- Before / After
- Talking Head
- Close-Up Demo
- Routine
- First Impression
- POV

## Core Angles

- Shade / Color
- Texture
- Finish
- Skin Concern
- Makeup Look
- Routine
- Transformation

## Beauty Human Realism

Prioritize:
- Realistic skin texture
- Natural facial expressions
- Realistic makeup application
- Accurate hand anatomy
- Believable product interaction
- Realistic reflections
- Natural lighting on skin
- Consistent texture and finish

Avoid:
- Plastic-looking skin
- Excessively perfect makeup
- Unnatural facial movement
- Floating products or tools
- Impossible application behavior

## Product Consistency

Preserve:
- Packaging
- Brand
- Product shape
- Shade
- Color
- Texture
- Finish
- Application state
