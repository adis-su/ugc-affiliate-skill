# Fashion Niche

## Purpose

Define Fashion-specific creative and realism rules for UGC Affiliate content.

## Core Formats

- Silent Mirror Selfie
- Outfit Showcase
- Try-On
- GRWM
- Talking Head
- POV
- Lifestyle
- Before / After

## Core Angles

- Outfit Inspiration
- Styling
- Fit Check
- Occasion-Based
- Trend
- Wardrobe Essential

## Fashion Human Realism

Prioritize:
- Natural body anatomy
- Natural posing
- Realistic garment fit
- Fabric physics
- Natural folds and wrinkles
- Realistic mirror reflection
- Smartphone camera behavior
- Imperfect framing
- Natural movement

Avoid:
- Fashion-editorial posing unless explicitly requested
- Studio-commercial lighting
- Unrealistically perfect skin or anatomy
- Floating or fused garments
- Impossible fabric behavior

## Product Consistency

Preserve:
- Garment color
- Pattern
- Material
- Cut
- Fit
- Logo and visible branding
- Accessories
- Clothing state across scenes
