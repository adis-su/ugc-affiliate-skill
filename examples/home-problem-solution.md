# Home Example — Problem → Solution

This fixture demonstrates a Home UGC concept focused on visible problem solving.

## Input

- Niche: Home
- Product: Example desk organizer
- Product URL: Not provided
- Campaign Objective: Affiliate Conversion
- Campaign Stage: Conversion
- CTA: Shop Now
- Creator: Rositasari
- Format: Problem → Solution
- Angle: Organization
- Duration: 10 sec
- Scene Count: 4
- Platform: Shopee Video

## Expected Output

### Creative Summary

- Creative Concept: A cluttered desk is shown first, then Rositasari places the organizer, sorts a few items into it, and reveals the cleaner workspace.

### Scene Plan

#### Scene 01

- Purpose: Show the organization problem.
- State: Several ordinary desk items are scattered on the workspace.
- Creator Action: Briefly points attention toward the clutter.
- Product Interaction: Organizer is not yet in use.
- Behavior Cue: Small pause and inspection.
- Environment: Same desk and room throughout.
- Camera State: Vertical smartphone view of the desk.
- Continuity Lock: Desk geometry, lighting, item placement until moved.
- Transition Intent: Bring organizer into the workspace.

#### Scene 02

- Purpose: Introduce the solution.
- State: Organizer is placed on the desk beside the scattered items.
- Creator Action: Places organizer naturally.
- Product Interaction: Organizer contacts desk surface.
- Behavior Cue: Small repositioning adjustment.
- Environment: Same desk and room.
- Camera State: Similar framing.
- Continuity Lock: Organizer shape, size, material, color, desk geometry.
- Transition Intent: Begin sorting.

#### Scene 03

- Purpose: Demonstrate organization.
- State: Several desk items are being placed into the organizer.
- Creator Action: Picks up and sorts items one by one.
- Product Interaction: Items enter organizer compartments.
- Behavior Cue: Natural hand repositioning.
- Environment: Same desk.
- Camera State: Slight reframing to keep hands and organizer visible.
- Continuity Lock: Organizer identity and room geometry.
- Transition Intent: Finish sorting and reveal result.

#### Scene 04

- Purpose: Show the improved state.
- State: Desk is visibly more organized with the organizer in use.
- Creator Action: Hand withdraws and briefly presents the organized desk.
- Product Interaction: Organizer remains stable on desk.
- Behavior Cue: Small final inspection.
- Environment: Same desk and room.
- Camera State: Slightly wider vertical framing.
- Continuity Lock: Organizer identity, desk geometry, remaining object placement.
- Transition Intent: None.
