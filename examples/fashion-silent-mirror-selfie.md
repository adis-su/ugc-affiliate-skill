# Fashion Example — Silent Mirror Selfie

This fixture demonstrates the expected output structure for a short silent Fashion UGC concept.

## Input

- Niche: Fashion
- Product: Example everyday knit top
- Product URL: Not provided
- Campaign Objective: Product Discovery
- Campaign Stage: Consideration
- CTA: View Product
- Creator: Rositasari
- Format: Silent Mirror Selfie
- Angle: Fit Check
- Duration: 6 sec
- Scene Count: 3
- Platform: TikTok

## Expected Output

### Creative Summary

- Creative Concept: Rositasari casually checks the fit of the knit top in a bedroom mirror, makes a small adjustment, then shows the finished silhouette.

### Scene Plan

#### Scene 01

- Purpose: Establish the outfit and product.
- State: Rositasari faces the mirror with the knit top clearly visible.
- Creator Action: Holds the phone naturally and looks at the outfit.
- Product Interaction: The top is worn normally.
- Behavior Cue: Brief visual inspection.
- Environment: Ordinary bedroom mirror area.
- Camera State: Vertical smartphone framing with slight handheld imperfection.
- Continuity Lock: Creator identity, knit top appearance, wardrobe, bedroom geometry.
- Transition Intent: Shift attention toward the garment fit.

#### Scene 02

- Purpose: Demonstrate fit.
- State: Rositasari turns slightly to show the garment silhouette.
- Creator Action: Makes a small body turn and lightly adjusts the hem.
- Product Interaction: Fabric moves naturally with the body.
- Behavior Cue: Brief glance at the adjusted area.
- Environment: Same bedroom mirror area.
- Camera State: Similar framing with minor natural phone movement.
- Continuity Lock: Same creator, garment, wardrobe, environment, and phone orientation.
- Transition Intent: Return toward a clear final outfit view.

#### Scene 03

- Purpose: Provide final fit evidence and soft product focus.
- State: Rositasari faces the mirror again with the garment settled.
- Creator Action: Holds the pose naturally for a moment.
- Product Interaction: Top remains worn and settled.
- Behavior Cue: Small posture adjustment.
- Environment: Same bedroom mirror area.
- Camera State: Vertical smartphone framing, naturally imperfect.
- Continuity Lock: Same creator, garment, wardrobe, environment.
- Transition Intent: None.

Rositasari wearing the same example everyday knit top, standing naturally in front of a bedroom mirror and holding a smartphone for a vertical mirror selfie. The fabric follows the body naturally with realistic folds. Keep the same wardrobe, mirror, room geometry, phone orientation, and visual identity. Vertical smartphone framing, natural indoor light, believable UGC imperfections.

#### Scene 03 — Image Prompt

The same Rositasari and the same knit top in the same bedroom mirror setting, now facing the mirror again with the garment settled. Natural relaxed posture, subtle weight shift, clear full outfit visibility, consistent room geometry and wardrobe, vertical smartphone framing, natural indoor lighting, realistic anatomy and fabric behavior, ordinary human-made UGC appearance.

### Frame-to-Frame Video Prompts

#### Scene 01 → 02 — Video Prompt

Starting from the exact Scene 01 mirror-selfie state, Rositasari briefly inspects the outfit, shifts her weight, turns her body slightly, and brings one hand to the hem for a small adjustment. Her movement remains natural and restrained. The knit fabric follows the body with believable folds and settling. The phone stays mostly stable with a small natural handheld shift. End in the exact Scene 02 state without changing the creator, wardrobe, room geometry, or product appearance.

#### Scene 02 → 03 — Video Prompt

Starting from the exact Scene 02 state, Rositasari releases the hem, settles her posture, and turns back toward the mirror-facing position. The garment follows the movement naturally and remains the same in color, material, cut, and fit. The phone makes only a subtle natural adjustment. End in the exact Scene 03 state with the same creator, wardrobe, room, and camera orientation.

### Silent Behavior Script

notice → inspect → turn → adjust → reveal
