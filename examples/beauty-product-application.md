# Beauty Example — Product Application

This fixture demonstrates a spoken Beauty UGC concept while keeping visual prompts independent from the full dialogue.

## Input

- Niche: Beauty
- Product: Example lip product
- Product URL: Not provided
- Campaign Objective: Product Consideration
- Campaign Stage: Consideration
- CTA: Check the Product
- Creator: Rositasari
- Format: Product Application
- Angle: Shade / Color
- Duration: 8 sec
- Scene Count: 3
- Platform: Instagram Reels

## Expected Output

### Creative Summary

- Creative Concept: Rositasari shows the lip product, applies a small amount, and brings the result closer to camera so the shade is easy to inspect.

### Scene Plan

#### Scene 01

- Purpose: Establish product and shade context.
- State: Product is held near the face with packaging visible.
- Creator Action: Looks at the product, then toward camera.
- Product Interaction: Holds the closed product.
- Behavior Cue: Small inspection glance.
- Environment: Ordinary bedroom or vanity area.
- Camera State: Vertical smartphone close-up.
- Continuity Lock: Creator identity, product packaging, environment.
- Transition Intent: Open product and prepare application.

#### Scene 02

- Purpose: Show application.
- State: Product is open and being applied naturally.
- Creator Action: Applies a controlled amount to the lips.
- Product Interaction: Applicator contacts the lips.
- Behavior Cue: Concentrated but relaxed expression.
- Environment: Same vanity area.
- Camera State: Similar close framing.
- Continuity Lock: Same creator, product, makeup state, environment.
- Transition Intent: Finish application and reveal shade.

#### Scene 03

- Purpose: Show shade result.
- State: Product is applied and visible on the lips.
- Creator Action: Moves slightly closer to camera.
- Product Interaction: Product remains in hand or nearby without changing identity.
- Behavior Cue: Subtle satisfied-looking reaction.
- Environment: Same vanity area.
- Camera State: Slight natural phone reframing.
- Continuity Lock: Same creator, product, makeup state, environment.
- Transition Intent: None.

### Image Prompts

#### Scene 01 — Image Prompt

Rositasari holding the same example lip product near her face in an ordinary vanity setting, closed packaging clearly visible, looking between the product and the smartphone camera. Natural facial expression, realistic skin texture, accurate hand anatomy, vertical smartphone close-up, natural indoor lighting, ordinary UGC appearance, no commercial beauty campaign polish.

#### Scene 02 — Image Prompt

The same Rositasari in the same vanity setting with the same lip product now open, applying a controlled amount naturally to her lips. Realistic hand and applicator placement, believable product application, natural skin and lip texture, restrained facial movement, vertical smartphone close framing, consistent lighting and environment.

#### Scene 03 — Image Prompt

The same Rositasari and same lip product in the same vanity setting after application. The shade is visibly present on the lips without exaggerated saturation. She is slightly closer to the smartphone camera for inspection, with natural skin texture and subtle facial expression. Preserve packaging identity, makeup state, environment, and camera continuity.

### Frame-to-Frame Video Prompts

#### Scene 01 → 02 — Video Prompt

Starting from the exact Scene 01 state, Rositasari opens the lip product, brings the applicator toward her lips, and applies a controlled amount with natural hand movement. The packaging remains consistent, the application follows a believable path, and her facial movement stays restrained. The phone remains mostly stable with slight handheld drift. End in the exact Scene 02 application state.

#### Scene 02 → 03 — Video Prompt

Starting from the exact Scene 02 application state, Rositasari finishes the application, lowers the applicator, and moves slightly closer to the phone for shade inspection. The product and makeup state remain consistent, skin texture stays natural, and the camera reframes subtly rather than making a cinematic zoom. End in the exact Scene 03 state.

### Spoken Script

- Scene 01: “Aku lagi lihat shade ini dulu.”
- Scene 02: “Coba aku pakai sedikit.”
- Scene 03: “Kelihatan juga warnanya.”

## Validation Notes

- Speech is separated from visual prompts.
- The example uses no unsupported product claims.
- Application has a physical sequence.
- The shade is described visually without inventing a named color.
